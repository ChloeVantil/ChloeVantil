Hey there!

My name is Chloe and I am a Sound Designer / Audio Engineer at XBOX Studios. I received my BFA in Sound Design at SCAD (Savannah College of Art and Design) in 2019.

I started a sound design internship at Warner Bros. in 2019 which turned into three years of working in their broadcast division as a Sound Designer for on-air content and Audio Engineer for voice-overs. I mixed commercials and some on-air documentaries for TBS, TCM (Turner Classic Movies), TNT, the NBA, and Cartoon Network.

In 2022 I made the transition to game audio working as a Sound Designer at Monolith Productions on their upcoming Wonder Woman title. My responsibilities included foley, combat, and UI design. I was most excited to incorporate "healing" tones into my sound design for the game. My concept for Wonder Woman's combat design came from the idea that Wonder Woman stands for equity and unity, so incorporating the healing frequencies in the Solfeggio scale would positively impact the player in combat and throughout the game. Unfortunately that game was never released, the studio shut down so I am unable to share my work for that project.

My current role at XBOX Studios supports an array of audio responsibilities including production and post-production audio, sound design for games and linear media, mixing, composition, creating AI voices using Azure, and game audio evaluations for Microsoft game devs.
